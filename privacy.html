Double Coverage Clips — Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026 Scope TikTok Content Posting API
01

What this covers

Double Coverage Clips ("the App") is a personal automation tool that publishes edited podcast highlight clips to one connected TikTok account. This policy explains what the App accesses through TikTok's API and how that access is used.

02

What is accessed

DataSourcePurpose
OAuth access & refresh tokensTikTok login flowAuthorize video uploads to the connected account
Open ID, username, avatarTikTok profile scopeConfirm which account is connected before posting
Upload status per videoTikTok Content Posting APIConfirm a clip published successfully
03

What is never accessed

The App never receives or stores a TikTok password. It does not read direct messages, contacts, browsing activity, or any account data outside the scopes listed above. No payment or financial information is collected.

04

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally, on the device the App runs on, encrypted at rest by the device's own filesystem protections. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party server, analytics platform, or ad network. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

05

Retention & deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the App is actively used to post on the connected account. Disconnecting the App from TikTok's account settings immediately invalidates its access; the operator deletes the corresponding local token on the same request.
