Acceptance

Double Coverage Clips ("the App") is a video-clipping and posting tool operated for the purpose of publishing short-form highlight clips to TikTok. By connecting a TikTok account to the App, the account owner agrees to these terms.

02

What the app does

The App selects short segments from podcast source video, formats them for vertical short-form video, overlays a provided watermark, and publishes the result to the connected TikTok account. No content is generated by AI narration or synthetic media — every clip is an edited excerpt of real recorded footage.

03

Source material & licensing

Clips are cut exclusively from footage made available for this purpose under the Double Coverage Podcast's clipping campaign, run through Reach / Content Rewards (Whop). The App does not clip, repost, or redistribute any footage outside the scope of that campaign's terms.

The App requests only the TikTok API scopes required to publish video on the connected account's behalf. No TikTok password is ever collected, viewed, or stored — access runs entirely through TikTok's OAuth flow, and the account owner can revoke it at any time from their TikTok account settings.

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App in TikTok's account settings, or by request to the contact below. The App may stop operating for a given account if TikTok's platform rules or the source campaign's terms are no longer met.
